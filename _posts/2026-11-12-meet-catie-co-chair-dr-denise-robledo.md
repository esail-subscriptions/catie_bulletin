Dr. Denise Robledo, Director of Digital Learning &amp; Academic Innovations at Texas A&amp;M University-Corpus Christi, serves as the CATIE Co-Chair. Drawing on her experience in digital learning, instructional innovation, and system-wide collaboration, Dr. Robledo is helping lead efforts to strengthen connections among faculty, instructional designers, academic technologists, and other professionals across The Texas A&amp;M University System. 

**Dr. Robledo is also the chair of the CATIE Annual Conference in 2027.**  The conference theme,**"[Roaring into the Future: One System, Many Solutions, Rising Together,](https://hub.tamus.edu/catie/events/conference/ "https://hub.tamus.edu/catie/events/conference/")"** celebrates the power of collaboration across the A&amp;M System as institutions address emerging technologies, evolving student needs, and new opportunities for innovation in teaching and learning. Faculty, academic technology professionals, instructional designers, and digital learning leaders are encouraged to participate by submitting proposals and volunteering to support the conference.
